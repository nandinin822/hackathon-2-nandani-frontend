System Architecture

How AegisCare Works

Built upon a robust two-tier architecture that combines zero-lag Edge kinematic pose tracking with cloud multimodal reasoning.

01

MediaPipe BlazePose Lite

Extracts 33 spatial keypoints at over 30 FPS purely on CPU without discrete GPU requirements. Computes 3D joint topology in real-time.

  • 33 3D normalized landmarks
  • Model size: ~5.6 MB
  • Sub-millisecond pose latency
02

BiLSTM Neural Ensemble

A dual-model Bidirectional LSTM ensemble trained on benchmark fall datasets. Processes sliding temporal windows to distinguish true falls from sitting or bending.

  • 30-frame temporal window (2.0s)
  • 12 kinematic velocity & inclination features
  • FSM alarm gating state machine
03

Multimodal Gemini 3.8 Flash

Provides deep contextual verification for subtle elder hazards — identifying dropped mobility aids, slumped posture in armchairs, and prolonged inactivity.

  • Multi-hazard semantic recognition
  • Structured JSON safety classifications
  • Natural-language caregiver digests
04

Automated Family Alerting

Direct push notifications through Telegram Bot API delivering keyframe photo snapshots, timestamps, and confidence scores with 15s duplicate suppression.

  • Instant photo & caption delivery
  • Anti-spam cooldown timer
  • Zero 24/7 video watching needed

Safety & Compliance Statement

AegisCare AI is an assistive eldercare monitoring tool designed to enhance caregiver awareness. It is not an FDA-approved medical device, diagnostic system, or primary life-support monitor. Fall probabilities ($P_{\text{fall}}$) represent heuristic statistical likelihoods from computer vision classifiers and must not be interpreted as definitive clinical measurements. In case of acute medical emergencies, always dial local emergency medical services immediately.
